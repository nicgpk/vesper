# v173 · Three new scenery themes

Dunes remains the default. Alpine brings a blue glacier and lilac actions; Basalt has grayscale volcanic cliffs, gray glass and silver actions (semantic success/error colors remain for status); Salt Flats uses rose scenery with jade accents. All three reuse the existing frosted-glass surfaces and Contrast mode. Collection covers and the nested appearance example follow the active theme. Phone stages use 768 px WebPs, and all three covers include 384 px and 768 px options.

The backdrops are original generations from the built-in image tool, prompted as: a blue-white hanging glacier at lavender twilight; neutral black-and-white volcanic cliffs under overcast light; dusky rose polygonal salt crust under lilac skies. Optimized derivatives are in apps/storybook/vesper/site/assets/nature-{alpine,basalt,salt-flats}*.webp.
